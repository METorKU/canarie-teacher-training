**Summary:** You give a clear overview and report the figures accurately. Make the detail paragraphs more comparative, especially by quantifying the biggest changes, to make your key features sharper.

## Task Achievement / Task Response
**Strengths**
- Your overview, 'internet access increased in all four countries', identifies the main overall trend.
- You select a key contrast in 'Country C grew the most, while Country D changed only a little'.
- You report accurate end-point comparisons in 'Country A reached 91%' and 'Country C also increased dramatically to 77%'.
**Improvements**
- Your overview says growth was different but does not quantify the contrast. Try: 'All countries rose, but Country C recorded the largest increase, from 12% to 77%, whereas Country D grew by only 15 percentage points', which highlights the key feature precisely.
- Your sentence 'By 2010, Country A had risen to 52% and Country C doubled to 25%' omits the figures for the two slower countries. Try: 'By 2010, Countries B and D had reached 60% and 68%, so they still led A and C', which strengthens the comparison.
- The statement 'Country D went from the first place to the last place' is clear but informal. Try: 'Country D fell from the highest proportion in 2005 to the lowest in 2020', which is more appropriate for Task 1.

## Coherence and Cohesion
**Strengths**
- The introduction, overview and two detail paragraphs create a logical report structure.
- The contrast marker 'On the other hand' clearly introduces the slower growth of Countries B and D.
- The reference 'the following years' helps move the reader from the first period to the later data.
**Improvements**
- The repeated pattern 'Country A' and 'Country C' makes the detail paragraph less smooth. Try: 'Both nations then expanded rapidly, with A reaching 91% and C climbing to 77%', which uses referencing to avoid repetition.
- The sentence beginning 'In 2005' lists three figures before explaining their relationship. Try: 'Country D led in 2005 at 60%, 15 percentage points ahead of B and double A's figure', which makes the comparison easier to follow.
- Your final sentence contains several ideas joined by 'and' and 'so'. Try: 'In contrast, B and D grew more modestly, finishing at 80% and 75% respectively. As a result, D ranked last by 2020', which gives clearer progression.

## Band 8 model answer
The table compares the proportions of households with internet access in four countries in 2005, 2010, 2015 and 2020.

Overall, internet access rose in every country. Country C experienced the most dramatic growth, while Country D increased only modestly and moved from first place at the start of the period to last place by 2020. Country A finished with the highest figure.

In 2005, Country D was the clear leader, with 60% of homes connected to the internet. This was followed by Country B at 45% and Country A at 30%, while Country C had by far the smallest share, at just 12%. Five years later, D and B still had the highest proportions, at 68% and 60% respectively. Meanwhile, access in A had risen to 52%, and the figure for C had more than doubled to 25%.

Growth was particularly strong in the final decade for Countries A and C. The proportion in A climbed from 52% in 2010 to 91% in 2020, overtaking all other countries. C also rose sharply, reaching 77%. By contrast, the increases in B and D were comparatively small: B finished at 80%, while D reached 75%. Thus, despite starting with the highest level of access, D had the lowest percentage at the end of the period.
