**Summary:** This is a clear, appropriately formal complaint that covers every bullet point. To improve further, use a little more precise complaint vocabulary and state the requested action with an even firmer deadline.

## Task Achievement / Task Response
**Strengths**
- Your purpose is immediately clear in 'I am writing to complain about a coffee machine'.
- You cover the fault, the phone call and the requested outcome in separate, well-developed paragraphs.
- Your request 'replace the machine with a new one or give me a full refund' is specific and practical.
**Improvements**
- Your detail 'it has not worked properly since the first day' could specify the fault sooner. Try: 'Since purchase, the machine has failed to dispense water and has made an unusually loud noise', which gives the manager a clearer summary.
- Your requested action could be more decisive. Try: 'I would appreciate either a replacement or a full refund within seven days of receiving this letter', which states the deadline more firmly.
- You could add a reference to the purchase evidence. Try: 'I enclose a copy of the receipt and can return the machine in its original packaging at your convenience', which makes the request easier to process.

## Coherence and Cohesion
**Strengths**
- Each paragraph has a clear purpose: purchase, fault, telephone contact, then resolution.
- The transition 'However, nobody has contacted me since then' clearly contrasts the promise with the result.
- The closing request follows logically from the explanation of the failed service.
**Improvements**
- The sentence beginning 'When I switched the machine on' has several linked details. Try: 'When I switched it on, the indicator light worked, but no water came through. After several minutes, the machine began to make a loud noise', which improves flow.
- Your phrase 'the same thing' is vague. Try: 'the same assurance that a technician would contact me', which gives a precise reference.
- The phrase 'I would therefore like' is good, but can link more directly to the failed contact. Try: 'As no technician has contacted me, I now request a replacement or a full refund', which strengthens the logical connection.

## Lexical Resource
**Strengths**
- You use appropriate formal complaint language in 'customer service department', 'arrange a repair' and 'full refund'.
- The phrase 'I would be grateful if you could reply within seven days' is polite and suitable for the recipient.
- The word 'technician' precisely identifies the person expected to resolve the fault.
**Improvements**
- The phrase 'the problem did not disappear' is understandable but informal. Try: 'the fault persisted', which is more precise and formal.
- Your wording 'make a loud noise' can be more specific. Try: 'emit a loud mechanical noise', which describes the issue more professionally.
- The phrase 'a member of staff' is slightly general. Try: 'a customer-service representative', which is more exact in this context.

## Grammatical Range and Accuracy
**Strengths**
- You use present perfect accurately in 'nobody has contacted me since then'.
- The complex sentence 'She told me that a technician would call me back within two days to arrange a repair' is controlled and clear.
- Formal conditional phrasing in 'I would be grateful if you could reply' is accurate.
**Improvements**
- Your sentence 'the light came on but no water came through' can be punctuated more clearly. Try: 'The indicator light came on, but no water flowed through the machine', which separates the clauses correctly.
- Your phrase 'although I called again last week and was promised the same thing' is slightly compressed. Try: 'Although I called again last week, I was given the same promise', which makes the contrast clearer.
- Your request can use a more formal modal. Try: 'I would be grateful if you could confirm how you intend to resolve this matter', which maintains an appropriately professional tone.

## Band 8 model answer
Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to complain about a coffee machine that I purchased from your shop on 3 March. The appliance has been faulty from the first day of use, and my attempts to resolve the matter through your customer-service department have been unsuccessful.

When I switch the machine on, the indicator light operates normally, but no water is dispensed. In addition, after several minutes the appliance begins to make a loud mechanical noise. I followed all of the instructions in the manual, including cleaning the water tank and checking the filter, but the fault has persisted.

I contacted your customer-service department by telephone on 5 March. I was told that a technician would call me within two days to arrange a repair. As I received no call, I contacted the department again last week and was given the same assurance. To date, however, nobody has contacted me.

I therefore request either a replacement machine or a full refund. I still have the receipt and the original packaging, and I can return the item as required. I would appreciate a written response within seven days of receiving this letter.

Yours faithfully,

[Name]
